## Part 1 (one 5-hour window): learning report and difficulty by subject
- **Learning report in Grown-ups:**
  - what the child practised this week in Vietnamese, English, Maths, Science and Money;
  - what went well;
  - one game suggested for next week;
  - shareable as a picture. It stays on the device.
- **Difficulty by subject:** the level follows the child per subject (strong in Maths, new to English). The parent's own setting still wins.
- **Checks:** new `learningreport` and `subjectlevel`, plus `phone`, `bilingual`.

## Part 2: Lumi as the guide
- Lumi welcomes new children, explains new things, carries the week's news and is the voice of the Simple screen.
- Lines are recorded in EN and VI with the female voice.
- Use the friends' code builder or the gallery pictures for Lumi. Do not wait for the 3D model.
- **Checks:** new `lumiguide`, plus `simple`, `voicecover`.

## Part 3: play together and gentle play time
- **"Chơi cùng bố mẹ / Play with Mum and Dad":** a list of games two people can play on one device, with a short guide for the grown-up.
- **Gentle play time:** a parent sets the play time. Lumi says a soft goodbye ("Hẹn mai chơi tiếp nhé!"), never cutting a game off in the middle.
- **Checks:** new `playtogether` and `playtime`, plus `phone`.
