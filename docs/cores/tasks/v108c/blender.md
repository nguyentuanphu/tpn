## Faces with feeling (Release update, 6 Nov 2026 AEDT)
What the update asked (end of `blender-brief.md` on `origin/integrate/v108`) and what was done:

- **The films' faces (`js/film.ts`)**: the lips that follow the words are kept; while quiet, a soft blink (half, closed, half) every 2 to 6 s, each actor at their own moment, and between blinks the eyes turn to whoever is speaking (`lookL`/`lookR`). Story moods `cry` (the sad face; tears in the Blender pages) and `sly` (the curious face, one brow up); `surprised` is the o face. The blink and the glances wait for the soft faces (they start once `avatar.ts` has the `half` face), so this branch alone looks as before.
- **Found and fixed on the way (for Release):** on `work/v108e-faces` alone the film actors' faces are blank: its `animate()` moves a painted face picture as if it were the 12-face sheet, and a film actor's face is one picture. A film actor is now not `painted` (`js/film.ts`, one line), so the film drives its own face. Seen in the trial merge: faces show, blink and glance.
- **Feelings on the pages:** each tale page's picture is the moment that shows its feeling best (`scene-export.mts at=feel`: five moments, the people's moods scored, sad and surprised first), or the moment the film names with `S.still(t)`. In Blender every actor shows the face of its mood, quiet smiling people look at the speaker, a crying person has tears.
