# Helper brief: friends (the four new characters and the villagers)

Branch `work/v108b-friends`. Plan: `docs/plans/new-friends-characters.md` (read it whole; the owner's answers are at the end). Designs: `docs/artifacts/sparkle-valley-characters/` (gallery), Blender scripts in `tools/blender/` (`lumi.py`, `lumi-rig.py`, `vehicles.py`; `blender -b -P tools/blender/lumi.py -- <dir> <who> game glb` makes a .glb; Blender 4.2 is at /opt/blender-dl/blender-4.2.9-linux-x64/blender or install it as `docs/cores/tasks/v108/lumi.md` says). Characters: boys Finn and Sol, girls Clara (was Flora) and Lumi.

Do, in this order:
1. **Light models:** each friend from the Blender script to about 300–500 KB: 6–8k triangles, one 512 px texture, mood atlas, shared skeleton with the game's joint names, meshopt-compressed .glb in `models/`. A loader (three.js GLTFLoader from our vendored three) that loads after Welcome, never in the first download (check `startup`, firstKB limit 1071). Offline list (`sw.ts`) and the Wi-Fi pack. Fallback: if a model can't load, the child is shown by the code builder in that friend's colours.
2. **Welcome:** name, "boy or girl?" (two big cards), choose your friend (the two of that gender, turning slowly, a spoken hello each in EN and VI), skin tone (5), then age/start screen/difficulty as today. The photo is only for the round picture, not the 3D face.
3. **Existing players:** `look.set` boy → Finn, girl → Clara (guess from clothes when missing, as `js/content/shop.ts` does); skin tone carries over; nothing else changes; the old look fields stay in the save. A one-time card "Các bạn mới đã đến! / New friends are here!" to switch to the other friend. Online friends on older versions are shown as the default friend of their gender.
4. **Pad:** in the Bag, section "Nhân vật & Trang phục / Character & Outfits" with a row of the two friends of the child's gender (free switch). Gender change only in Grown-ups › Children › Edit. Farm wardrobe and the shop mirror open the same section.
5. **Wearables:** hats (all 18 shop and 4 passport and tester hats), neck, back, feet, and a new glasses slot fit all four friends via one attach point each; outfits (4 per friend, from the gallery wardrobe) replace top+bottom and are sold in the shop; tops and bottoms already bought become a same-colour outfit for the child's friend, free, with the note "Áo cũ đã thành bộ đồ mới / Old clothes became new outfits". New 2D shop pictures from the models. Items in the hand (compass, flower staff, sun crystal, star lantern) earned from each friend's quests, not sold.
6. **Villagers (owner's choice A):** reshape the code builder in `js/avatar.ts` to the new style (proportions, heights children 1.6 / grown-ups 2.0 / grandparents 1.8 per WORLD-011, smaller eyes, soft cheeks, felt-look hair, seven moods), so the 11 named villagers, crowds and Studio villagers match the friends and stay light (perf 420/490 draws, battery still copies, longplaymem). Studio's villager and story-character pages get the new look choices; old Studio villagers map to the closest look.
7. **Vehicles and films:** `seatRider` uses the friends' joints; films (`js/film.ts`) and the lantern story use the chosen friend; the tale theatre's 2D people are redrawn to match. (The story helper does costumes; you do the people.)
8. **Checks:** new `newfriends`, `friendmigrate`, `friendswap`, `wearfit`; existing `perf`, `battery`, `longplaymem`, `startup`, `phone`, `vehicles`, `stories`, `films`, `shop`, `simple` must pass.

Work with the games helper on the pad (they add nothing to the Bag) and with the story helper on films (they change costumes of tale people, you change the people builder). Coordinate through your status files.
