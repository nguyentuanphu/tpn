# Three short launch videos (15 to 30 seconds)

Made by the v1.07 helper "launch", 2 Nov 2026 AEDT. Vertical 1080 x 1920 (the stills are 720 x 1280 phone screens:
scale up, or take them again at `DPR = 3` in `capture.mts`). Each video is made twice: fully in Vietnamese and fully
in English (voice and captions), like `marketing/ads-r82/`. The pictures are real game screens in `shots/`
(`<name>-en.jpg`, `<name>-vi.jpg`), our own art only.

Who makes them: Release, 20 Dec 2026 to 10 Jan 2027 (`PLAN.md`), with the ad kit in `marketing/source/` (stills,
voice, music and render steps: `marketing/ads-r82/README.md`, "Making them again"). Voices: Azure (en-AU Natasha,
vi-VN Hoài My), recorded by a chat that has `AZURE_SPEECH_KEY`. No child's name is spoken (HANDOFF rule 11).

Motion for every still: slow zoom in (100 to 108 percent) or a slow pan; a soft "sparkle" wipe between shots; the
game's own jingle quietly under the voice. Last shot always: logo, sparklevalley.au, and the promise line.

`[DATA]`: the promise line waits for the owner's decision on children's data (`PLAN.md`, "Before we promise"). Until
then: "Free · No ads · Plays offline" / "Miễn phí · Không quảng cáo · Chơi được khi không có mạng".
