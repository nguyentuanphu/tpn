## English (send this first; libraries usually work in English)

**Subject:** Free bilingual Vietnamese-English learning game for your young readers

Dear [Librarian's name],

I am a Vietnamese-Australian parent in [suburb]. I made **Sparkle Valley** (sparklevalley.au), a free learning game for
children aged 2 to 8 in Vietnamese and English, with every word read aloud. Children trace letters, read first words,
count, learn about money, hear fairy tales read aloud and play traditional games such as Ô ăn quan.

It has no ads, needs no accounts `[DATA]`, plays offline after the first visit, and runs in the browser on library
tablets and computers.

Would the library be willing to:

- put the attached flyer on the community noticeboard or near the Vietnamese collection, and
- mention it to families at bilingual storytime or the January school-holiday programme?

I am happy to come and show it at a storytime session at no cost. The game sells nothing.

Thank you for all you do for our community's children.

Kind regards,
