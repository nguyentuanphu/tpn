# Tết fairs: Cabramatta, Bankstown, Footscray

When: the weekends around Tết, Sat 30 Jan to Sun 7 Feb 2027. Dates, places and stall rules: the owner checks with each
organiser (council or community association). **A paid stall, printing in bulk or anything paid waits for the owner.**
Free options to ask about first: a flyer on a community table, a friend's or school's stall.

What to bring: printed flyers (`flyer.html`, A4 or A5), a tablet with the game open (Simple screen for small children,
sound on, charged, offline voices kept), the stall card below printed A4 and stood up.

Never take children's names, photos or contact details at the stall. Parents scan nothing and sign up for nothing: they
just type sparklevalley.au.

## What to say in 20 seconds

**Tiếng Việt:** "Chào anh chị! Đây là Thung Lũng Lấp Lánh, trò chơi miễn phí cho bé 2 đến 8 tuổi, bằng tiếng Việt và
tiếng Anh, chữ nào cũng đọc thành tiếng. Bé tập chữ, đếm số, nghe truyện cổ tích, chơi ô ăn quan. Không quảng cáo, không
cần tài khoản. Anh chị mở sparklevalley.au là chơi được ngay. Bé muốn thử không?"

**English:** "Hello! This is Sparkle Valley, a free game for children aged 2 to 8 in Vietnamese and English, every word
read aloud. Children trace letters, count, hear fairy tales and play Ô ăn quan. No ads, no accounts. Just open
sparklevalley.au. Would your little one like a go?"

## Letting a child try at the stall

1. Open the game on the stall tablet with the Simple screen ("Big pictures" / "Hình to").
2. Let the child pick a game: Count With Me, Letter Hunt and Kite Flying are short and happy.
3. Hand the parent a flyer while the child plays. Answer questions from `facebook.md` ("Common questions").
4. Between children, nothing to clear: the stall tablet uses the made-up players only, never a child's name.
