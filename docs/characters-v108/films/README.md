# The five Blender films: stills (round v1.08c, blender helper)

Stills of the five films drawn in Blender (Cycles, 1280 x 720, 16 samples), shown here at 960 x 540. They come from the game film's own scenes (`tools/blender/scene-export.mts`), redrawn with the costume kit's people (`docs/characters-v108/costumes/`) by `tools/blender/storypage.py`.

- `<id>-intro.jpg` and `<id>-outro.jpg`: the four friends (Finn, Clara, Sol, Lumi) tell the story at the start and the end (`tools/blender/storyteller.py`). They are the only friends in the film.
- `<id>-p<k>-0.jpg` and `<id>-p<k>-1.jpg`: two moments of page k (a quarter and three quarters of the way through it).

Films: `banhchung` (Bánh Chưng Bánh Giầy), `chucuoi` (Chú Cuội), `tamcam` (Tấm Cám), `thanhgiong` (Thánh Gióng), `rainbowserpent` (The Rainbow Serpent).

The animated films are rendered later on a machine with a graphics card (owner, 5 Nov 2026 AEDT, "Animate later on a GPU"): see `docs/cores/tasks/v108c/blender-films-gpu.md`.
