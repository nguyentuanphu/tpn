# Master design

## The management model

Open any number, child/device record, content item or control and answer: what is it, where did it come from, what does it connect to, what can I change, when does it take effect, and what proves the result?

Five primary destinations: **Home; People; Game & website; Work; Money**. Settings is separate. Existing route `home` currently displays Today; proposed visible label Home does not require a duplicate destination. Marketing belongs within Game & website. Services and releases belong within Work. Function/privacy/access settings belong within Settings. Preserve old routes and return/filter/scroll context while migrating.

## Home: a short review, not all dashboards

Header: actual valley art, selected environment/build, reporting period and last refreshed status. Never mix preview and production silently. Show at most three actionable priorities, each with evidence, owner/next action if recorded and a destination. No invented urgency, due dates or composite health score.

Four to six quiet summary cards: reporting installations and reported play; experience problems; intended content readiness; configuration delivery; recorded costs when authorised. State denominators, units and period. A missing source is unavailable, not zero. People counts are reporting installations, not global children/customers.

Two principal graphs: reporting activity trend and a useful contextual comparison such as experience/delivery or recorded cost. Other graphs remain in their workspaces. Provide plain-language definitions, source tables, sample sizes, filters and freshness.

Below: content/control coverage and release gaps; meaningful recent work; source health. Money can retain its own month/basis, visibly labelled. Coverage requires a freshly audited intended inventory; do not use historic catalogue totals as a universal completeness denominator.

## People

List authorised reported child first names alongside device, game build, language/age band if supplied, last observation, reported play and problems. Detail: installation/profile context, compatible activity history, experience evidence, permitted issue links and current revision/hash receipts. Search and filters cannot bypass server grants. No name-based merging, fingerprint identity or website visitor-to-child inference. Private family membership, reported profile and installation are separate relationships. Show opted-out/missing/stale distinctions without exposing a private person's state to unauthorised users.

## Game & website: complete scope

Audit these 13 domains, preserving every real source item and recording unsupported controls:

1. Games, learning and mini-activities.
2. Stories, chapters and private family-authored content.
3. Quests, daily tasks and family missions.
4. Rewards, economy, purchases and unlock dependencies.
5. Festivals, schedules and shared visibility.
6. World, places, NPCs, buildings, transport, vendors and collectibles.
7. Multiplayer, social and connection features.
8. Voice, audio, images, videos and recording coverage.
9. Website pages, guides, acquisition and promotions.
10. People, profiles, installations and authorised family relationships.
11. Quality, reports, testing, work and releases.
12. Services, operations, provider feeds and resource usage.
13. Finance, recorded costs, budgets and commitments.

Each item uses stable `kind:id` and has: overview; player entry and dependencies; content/media; effective controls; website representation; usage/experience; work/release evidence; history/connections. Explicitly separate source existence, menu/runtime implementation, playability, control capability, observation and parent-friendly explanation. A catalogue row proves none of the other states.

Required item metadata: source/revision/hash; supported build; entry point; dependencies; audience/language/device/mode; current effective rule; actual write mechanism and consumer; website/media links; measurement definitions; tests; owner/next action where recorded; evidence status. Unknowns are explicit, never fabricated to turn coverage green.

## Work and services

Unify relevant issue evidence, assigned follow-up, content review, test evidence, release readiness, operation results and provider state. Do not claim automatic fixes or arbitrary execution. Existing bounded jobs retain idempotency, permissions and audit. Distinguish intended change, saved draft, approved source, published config, public readback, deployed build and device-reported receipt.

Required-control coverage states: working individually; group rule; source/build change; read-only; missing; unsupported. A required missing control remains a blocker unless the owner explicitly approves its release scope exception. No drag-to-deploy without actual release authority.

## Money

Manual-first ledger and imports with preview/commit, duplicates/corrections, exact integer minor units, original currency, explicit FX/tax, immutable history, invoice/cash basis, budgets and source coverage. Use readable currency graph labels while retaining exact source values. Missing provider invoices/FX/income do not become zero or a complete profit claim. No payments, bank integration, statutory-accounting completeness or true obligation due date is inferred. Provider cost feeds require real existing-service access and reconciliation.

## Shared visual and interaction contract

Reuse game fonts, illustrated art and shared colour tokens without importing game canvas layout. Stable header/body/actions; 44px adult targets and 64px under-five controls. EN/VI, keyboard focus, safe areas, long labels, error/loading/empty/offline states and phone/tablet/computer review are acceptance requirements. Use graphs where they support a decision; no dense wall of cards. This English review concept is not proof of completed bilingual runtime validation.

Zero, no samples, not collected, unknown, unavailable and stale are separate states. Every graph names calculation, denominator, time basis, eligible population, sample size, freshness, source, drilldown and limitations. Historical 26 graph contracts remain the starting register; additional marketing/resource graphs need explicit entries and schemas.
