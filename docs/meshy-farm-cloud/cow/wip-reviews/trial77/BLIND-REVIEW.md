# Independent blind cow review

**Verdict: REJECT** — r17-dev77, unapproved diagnostic candidate.

Inspected every one of the 44 original 1024 × 1024 PNGs individually with `view_image`: cheer + sleep, 11 angles, near + far. Read only those PNGs and `views.json`; no implementation, docs, logs, history or tests. No contact sheet, resized substitute, browser, Blender, network or comparison reference used. Only these two reports were written.

The expressions are readable, but the eye treatment is visibly insufficiently integrated with the furry head. This judgement does not reject inherited body/muzzle texture or faceting.

## F1: fur eyelids / eye silhouette (blocking)

Closed-eye crescents sit on conspicuous oval eye areas. On the white side, the oval has a pale rim and stretched, fan-like fur below/toward the nose; in profile it reads as an applied bulging plate rather than a naturally integrated furry eyelid. The black side also shows a circular/radial fur area around the crescent. This is a localized eye integration defect, not a rejection of general faceting.

Evidence: `cow-near-cheer-left.png`, `cow-near-sleep-left.png`, `cow-near-sleep-above.png`, `cow-near-cheer-right.png`, `cow-far-sleep-left.png`, `cow-far-cheer-p07.png`.

## F2: eye colour / readability (blocking)

The crescent on white fur is solid black while the corresponding crescent on the black patch is muted tan/grey-brown. Both are visible, but the tan line reads as a different material or exposed strip, especially beside the dark furry brow. The pair is graphically legible yet visually inconsistent and insufficiently integrated with the fur.

Evidence: `cow-near-cheer-front.png`, `cow-near-sleep-front.png`, `cow-far-cheer-front.png`, `cow-far-sleep-front.png`, `cow-near-sleep-p04.png`.

## F3: mouth / material integration (secondary)

Cheer mouth is a crisp dark burgundy oval with a bright flat pink tongue shape on the lower front of the muzzle. It communicates an open mouth, but looks like a flat graphic applied to the textured three-dimensional muzzle, with no convincing lip/depth transition. The texture and recessed nostrils make the mismatch especially visible. Sleep has no comparable conspicuous open-mouth graphic.

Evidence: `cow-near-cheer-front.png`, `cow-near-cheer-below.png`, `cow-near-cheer-neck.png`, `cow-far-cheer-front.png`, `cow-far-cheer-below.png`.

## F4: patch boundary (secondary)

A pale grey/white rim is visible along the lower/inner black facial patch near the eye and muzzle junction. The broad black/white patch itself remains readable; the concern is the local rim/seam around the eye area, not the existence or overall shape of the patch.

Evidence: `cow-near-cheer-above.png`, `cow-near-sleep-above.png`, `cow-near-cheer-p07.png`, `cow-near-sleep-p07.png`.

## Accepted observations and limits

- Cheer upturned crescents and sleep downturned crescents are distinguishable in visible frontal/three-quarter views.
- Head, horns, ears and muzzle retain a recognizable coherent cow silhouette.
- Pink muzzle and ear interiors, cream/black fur, tan horns and brown/gold collar remain coherent; recessed nostrils read clearly.
- General textured/faceted body and muzzle appearance is accepted as inherited style; no smoothing or replacement style is requested.
- No definite background-through hole in visible head, muzzle, jaw or neck was identified in any inspected image. Dark nostrils and cheer mouth are intentional-looking facial features, not evidence of holes.

- Image-only judgement: apparent eye rims/plates describe visible appearance, not underlying construction.
- Occluded face in back views and frame-cropped portions cannot establish hidden geometry integrity.
- No reference comparison was necessary; no claim of historical change or implementation cause is made.
