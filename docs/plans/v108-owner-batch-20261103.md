## The plan

**A. Lumi, refined (Blender, in `tools/blender/lumi.py`).**
- Changes:
  - a fringe over the forehead;
  - a smaller forehead and fuller cheeks;
  - softer lashes and eyebrows;
  - rolled sleeves, cream socks above the boots;
  - the star lantern with a soft glow;
  - leaf pockets;
  - the colours of the concept sheet.
- Renders from the front, side, back, three-quarter, and of the three faces (happy, curious, surprised).
- Nothing in the game changes. It waits for the owner's style choice and the Meshy key (Finn, Flora, Sol, Luna).

**B. The target chip for every child who has a companion.**
1. Check Henry's profile and the `saveGoal` switch in Studio, and confirm which one hides the chip.
2. When every hat is bought, the chip stays and shows the next goal instead of disappearing. The next goal is new companion hats (4 more, our own drawings, English and Vietnamese names). When there is truly nothing left, it shows a "Đủ mũ rồi! / All hats!" chip.
3. A check `companiongoal` covers the chip with no hat, some hats and all hats.

**C. Real English lessons.**
1. An English mode: a game opened from Học vui › Tiếng Anh plays in English (words, voice, instructions), whatever the child's language. A small Vietnamese hint sits under the instruction, and the child can tap to hear it in Vietnamese. First Sounds, Opposites, Listen and Touch, Story Choices, Lantern Letters, Word and Picture, Word Balloons and Rhyme Time support it.
2. New English games, made for children learning English:
   - Picture Words (animals, colours, numbers, family, food, body);
   - Listen and Choose;
   - Say and Match;
   - English Songs (ABC, numbers);
   - First Sentences ("I see a ...");
   - Greetings (hello, thank you, sorry).
3. Lines are recorded with Azure en-AU (the game's English voice). Lines with a child's name are never recorded.
4. The section card says "Tiếng Anh" with the English mode badge, so parents know.

**D. Three times as many maths and science games.**
- Maths goes from 14 to about 42. First the 7 held-back games are switched on (owner's choice, Studio). Then about 21 new ones:
  - Number Bonds; Take Away (subtraction); Skip Counting; Patterns; Shapes Hunt; Symmetry;
  - Heavier or Lighter; Full or Empty (volume); Pizza Fractions; Picture Graph; Calendar; Quarter Past;
  - Number Puzzle (kids' sudoku); Bigger Number Race; Doubles; Tens and Ones; Coin Count in AUD;
  - Missing Number; Shapes in 3D; Left and Right; Mental Maths Sprint.
- Science goes from 6 to about 18, with 12 new games:
  - Grow a Plant; Water Cycle; Day and Night; Four Seasons; Animal Homes; My Body;
  - Mixing Colours; Ice Melts (states of matter); Rainbow Light; Light the Bulb (circuits); Gears; Recycle Sort.
- Each game gets a guide film (the TV button), levels up to Talent, English and Vietnamese, our own drawings, a place in the valley (the map pin) and a check.
- In 3 batches: v1.08 (+10 maths, +4 science), v1.09 (+11 maths, +4 science), v1.10 (+4 science).

**E. A Bạn đồng hành tab in the Bag.**
- The pad's tabs become: Bản đồ, Ba lô, **Bạn đồng hành**, Cửa hàng, Nhiệm vụ, Hình dán.
- The new tab holds:
  - the companion: wear, feed, hats, the coin goal;
  - pets: adopt, feed, name;
  - companion hats.
- Ba lô keeps food, things bought, treasures, materials, clothes, kites and gliders.
- Checks on phone, tablet and computer (44 px touch targets, 64 px for under-5s).

**F. Simple screen.**
1. Every Play game shows. A locked game appears as its own card with a padlock and what to do ("Chơi 3 trò để mở"), not folded into one padlock. Held-back games stay one "Coming soon" stop (the owner's Studio switch decides).
2. The top row becomes: name, Back, **Valley**, Sound, Home, Grown-ups. Valley leaves the bottom row.
3. The game buttons become two big buttons by kind:
   - **Học vui** holds everything about learning: Học (numbers, words, reading, shapes, me, money) and Học vui (Vietnamese, English, maths, science).
   - **Vui chơi** holds folk games, animals and music, drawing, places and thinking games.
   - Stories and Places stay.
4. The check `simplefirst` / `simplegames` is extended: every switched-on Play game is reachable on the Simple screen.
